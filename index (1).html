Our Story

About codieflorist.id

Delivering Emotions Through Flowers

Founded with a passion for beauty and a belief that every moment deserves to be celebrated, codieflorist.id is a modern floral studio crafting premium bouquets and arrangements that speak louder than words.

We source only the freshest blooms from local Indonesian growers and international farms, ensuring every petal meets our exacting standards of quality and beauty.

From romantic roses to whimsical wildflower arrangements, each creation is handcrafted by our skilled florists with meticulous attention to detail and an unwavering commitment to excellence.
